This report summarizes the picture quality evaluation of the OLED display under test (DUT).
The peak luminance reached 1000 cd/m2 at low APL, but the auto brightness limiter (ABL)
reduced the sustained luminance to about 250 cd/m2 at 100% APL. The full-screen contrast
ratio was effectively infinite in a dark room, yet the ambient contrast ratio (ACR) dropped
significantly due to surface reflectance. We observed mura in the upper region and slight
image sticking after a two-hour static pattern; no permanent burn-in was detected.

Color gamut coverage was 99% DCI-P3 and the color accuracy (Delta E) stayed below 2 after
calibration. In the gray scale measurement, the gamma curve followed the target EOTF closely,
although black crush appeared in the near-black region and banding was visible in smooth
gradients. Gray-to-gray (GtG) response time was 1 ms, which minimized motion blur, but
overdrive caused visible overshoot on some transitions.

Viewing angle performance showed measurable color shift off-axis. Luminance uniformity was
within 90%, and no backlight bleeding was present because the panel is emissive. During
variable refresh rate (VRR) operation, gamma shift produced perceptible flicker in the
peripheral vision. The white point was close to D65, and metamerism was negligible.
